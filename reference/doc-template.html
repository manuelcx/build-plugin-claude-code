One instance of every component in the house doc style. Pictures come first, then the specifics: real names, real values, real failure shapes.

Tables and callouts

The simple table for dense comparisons and glossaries ("Words you will hear"). The first column is automatically bold. Callouts carry one emphasized point each; pick the border color by meaning.

Short and concrete.

Blue callout (default): guidance, context, what a term means.

Green callout: reassurance, a success sign, "that's the full cycle".

Amber callout: this will look scary but is normal, or a caveat.

Red callout: a real danger, such as secrets in public files.

Mini-grid left: pair two contrasting callouts side by side.

Mini-grid right: for example "it will ask" next to "how to check it worked".
